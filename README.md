# E-commerce Delivery & Customer Satisfaction Analysis

**Tools:** Excel, Python, SQL, Statistics, Power BI

## Business problem
Do late deliveries hurt customer satisfaction, and how can the
marketplace increase repeat purchases?

## Dataset
Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist (Kaggle),
~100K orders, 9 tables, 2016-2018.
Download: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce
Place the CSV files in data/raw/ on your computer.

## Business questions
1. How do orders and revenue change over time?
2. Which categories and states earn the most?
3. What percentage of orders arrive late, and where?
4. Do late deliveries lower review scores?
5. How many customers buy again?
6. What 3 actions should the business take?
